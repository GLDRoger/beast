# Unslop

Make the result specific to the person, product, and purpose. Diagnose clusters of patterns and their effect on the reader or workflow; one em dash, font, color, or card is not evidence of a problem.

## Choose the relevant mode

- **Prose:** [prose.md](references/prose.md) for writing, editing, docs, UI strings, essays, and marketing.
- **Visual:** [visual.md](references/visual.md) for generic layout, hierarchy, typography, decoration, interaction, and UI/copy combinations.
- **Mixed:** use both only when the request actually includes both. A prose edit does not authorize a redesign; a visual cleanup does not authorize changing the product's claims.

Use existing context, brand, and the user's restored wording. Ask only when a missing choice materially changes the work. Review requests produce findings without edits. Explicit cleanup requests include the relevant edits and verification, not an extra report-preview-confirm loop. A review invoked within an authorized build returns actionable findings to that build.

Preserve facts, genuine uncertainty, quotations, accessibility, functional states, and intentional style. Diagnostic word lists and examples are aids, not universal bans or mandatory scoring rubrics. Do not invent numbers, personal texture, urgency, or stronger claims to make text feel human.

Deliver the revised artifact, or the requested ranked critique, with only the explanation needed to understand consequential choices and evidence limits.
