# Growth

Own the requested product outcome, not a default startup checklist. Keep the audience, constraints, evidence standard, and requested deliverable in view across follow-ups.

## Select the right lens

- Ideation, shortlist selection, hackathons, or technical demos: read [idea-search.md](references/idea-search.md). Explore distinct useful concepts and screen them against the actual criteria.
- Business viability or customer validation: `~/.agents/skill-modules/startup-pressure-test/SKILL.md`.
- Competitor/product investigation: `~/.agents/skill-modules/teardown/SKILL.md` when its method fits.
- Product scope or a decision: use current context to state the outcome, viable alternatives, tradeoffs, recommendation, and next authorized deliverable. No separate briefing/interview skill is required.

Draft from known facts. Identify important assumptions and disconfirming evidence. Ask only for a missing fact that would materially change the work. Research current market, competitor, pricing, or eligibility claims before presenting them as verified.

## Package and distribute when requested

Load only relevant specialists from `~/.agents/skill-modules/`:

- Pricing or monetization: `pricing-page/SKILL.md`, `ship-credits/SKILL.md`
- Discoverability: `seo-ready/SKILL.md`, `aeo-ready/SKILL.md`
- Outreach: `cold-email/SKILL.md`, `cold-outreach-sequence/SKILL.md`, `segment-users/SKILL.md`, `ship-email/SKILL.md`
- Product words: `app-copy/SKILL.md`, `unslop/SKILL.md`, `readme/SKILL.md`, `changelog/SKILL.md`

Use the writing, frontend, code, or media workflow for the actual authorized deliverable. Carry assumptions and acceptance criteria through the handoff and integrate the result. Strategy advice alone does not finish a requested implementation; a strategy question alone does not authorize building or sending outreach.
